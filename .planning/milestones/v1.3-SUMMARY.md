## Key decisions

- Per-machine clones; SMB checkout is sync-only (ended the 329-file CRLF
  phantom-diff problem).
- Self-review demoted from verdict to input; judge ordering enforced
  mechanically (no scores → no judge spend).
- **Owner delegation (2026-06-10):** quality judgment belongs to the
  strongest available model at high effort (Fable 5 today; configurable via
  --judge-model/--judge-effort). Human blind-sampling is not a recurring
  gate. Recorded in VERIFY-BOUNCE-CALIBRATION.md.
- Threshold recalibration deferred until ~50 instrumented (v1.3+) runs.
