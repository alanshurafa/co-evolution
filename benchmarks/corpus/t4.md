A small bookkeeping system runs do-it-yourself payroll for two single-officer
corporations. Each pays one salaried officer on its own cadence — one monthly,
one twice a month. A script generates journal entries, pay stubs, and
year-to-date state; the money transfer itself is manual. Monthly runs work.
Nothing beyond the monthly cycle has been exercised: tax deposits, quarterly
employment-tax returns, unemployment filings, and year-end wage statements are
all still ahead, each with its own deadline and its own submission portal.

Plan the quarterly and year-end procedure. Cover the deadline calendar and how
it is tracked, the order of operations for each filing, reconciling the ledger
against what was actually deposited, catching a missed or late deposit early,
what evidence is retained per filing, and which steps stay manual.

Produce a plan of roughly 600 words.
