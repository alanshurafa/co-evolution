A volunteer effort captured a large set of soon-to-disappear websites. The
results now sit in several overlapping copies on one workstation: an original
capture tree, a full backup tree, and two deployment trees that have drifted
apart. Together they consume tens of gigabytes to serve a published archive a
small fraction of that size, almost none of it version-controlled. Some files
exist only in the backup tree. One irreplaceable crawl record sits in a sync
folder with no recorded checksum.

Plan the work to make this corpus safe, then reclaim the duplicated space.
Cover inventory and checksumming across trees, deciding which differences
matter, off-machine copies with a tested restore, a self-contained
redistributable package, and a deletion gate strict enough that nothing unique
is lost. State the ordering constraints and what must be verified before
anything is deleted.

Produce a plan of roughly 700 words.
