A regional trade association is holding a three-day conference for four hundred
attendees at a single hotel venue, eight weeks from now. The venue and the dates
are booked; nothing else is. The program has two parallel session tracks, one
evening reception, and a small exhibitor area. Two staff members and a handful
of volunteers will run it.

Plan the logistics from now through the closing session. Cover the countdown
schedule and what must be locked by when, registration and on-site check-in,
room setup and audio-visual needs per track, catering across three days
including dietary requirements, signage and wayfinding, speaker communication
and travel, volunteer assignments during the event, and a short list of the
things most likely to go wrong on site with a response for each.

Produce a plan of roughly 550 words.
