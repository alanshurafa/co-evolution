An order-processing service stores each customer's shipping address as five
loose columns on its orders table. The team wants those moved into a separate
addresses table joined by a foreign key, so one address can be reused across
orders and corrected in one place. The orders table holds roughly forty million
rows, the service writes to it continuously, and the largest tolerable
maintenance window is thirty minutes.

Plan the migration. Cover the sequence of schema changes, how the application
reads and writes during the transition, backfilling existing rows without
saturating the database, verifying that migrated data matches its source, the
cutover itself, and a rollback that still works after the backfill has started.
Say what you would measure before and after, and name the point of no return.

Produce a plan of roughly 650 words.
