A payments team has no written procedure for handling a production outage.
Today whoever notices a problem improvises: they page people by guessing, debug
in a shared chat room, and write nothing down until someone asks for a
postmortem days later. The team is twelve engineers spread across three time
zones, each carrying the on-call pager for a week at a time.

Plan the work to build their incident-response runbook. Cover severity
definitions and who decides them, how an incident is declared and who takes
command, the roles filled while it is open, update cadence to customers and to
executives, when to stop debugging and mitigate instead, handoff between time
zones during a long incident, and how the runbook gets exercised and kept
current. Include what the first thirty days of adoption should look like.

Produce a plan of roughly 700 words.
