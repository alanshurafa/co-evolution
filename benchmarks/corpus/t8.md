A working professional has twelve weeks to prepare for a certification exam
covering six subject areas of uneven difficulty. They can study about eight
hours a week on weekday evenings, plus one longer block each weekend. They own
the official study guide, a question bank of roughly a thousand practice
questions, and one full-length timed practice exam they have not used yet. Two
of the six areas are entirely new to them; the other four overlap with their day
job.

Plan the twelve weeks. Cover how time is allocated across the six areas, the
order topics are studied in, when practice questions are used instead of
reading, repeated return to weak areas, when to spend the full-length practice
exam and what to do with the result, the final week, and what to cut if they
fall two weeks behind.

Produce a plan of roughly 550 words.
