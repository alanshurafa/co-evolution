A workstation diagnostics toolkit just recovered a virtual machine that refused
to boot: a routine operating-system component-store cleanup restored the
hypervisor's firmware validation and the guest started normally. A follow-up
health scan still reports the component store as damaged but repairable, and
the last two quality updates failed with certificate and missing-payload
errors. The recovery is real; the underlying servicing defect is not fixed.

Plan how to repair the component store and prove it is repaired. Cover the
ordering of repair attempts from least to most destructive, sourcing matching
repair content when the local store cannot heal itself, what evidence
distinguishes a genuine repair from a symptom that merely stopped appearing,
rollback if a repair leaves the machine unbootable, and how the finished
procedure should be written down so the next operator can follow it.

Produce a plan of roughly 600 words.
