What belongs in the composite

Operational yield, not latent accuracy or an intelligence score. Show coverage and possible upper bounds. Protocols are separate; calibration screens, AI-judge rubric scores, archives and external leaderboards are excluded. Weights are an explicit reporting convention. Cost is not blended because historical accounting is incomplete.

Every current internal test has a dedicated score page and an evidence assessment. Archives are history, not extra trials. External site descriptions were checked against their official pages or linked project documentation on 2026-09-17.
